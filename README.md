# My Favorite Movies & Series

Here are some of my favorite movies and series along with why I love them:

---

### 🎬 Movies
* **The Amazing Spider-Man (TASM Duology):** This is my favorite among all the Spider-Man movies, and **Andrew Garfield** is my favorite Spider-Man.

### 📺 Anime & Manga
* **Naruto & Naruto Shippuden:** These are my all-time favorites because I've watched them since I was a kid. I used to watch them on our old TV back in my grandma's house.
* **Dragon Ball Z:** Just like *Naruto*, this is a childhood favorite that I grew up watching on the CDs my cousin bought.
* **Dragon Ball Super:** I included this because it feels nostalgic, and it's great seeing my favorite childhood show get another sequel.
* **Kingdom:** This is one of the very first mangas I ever read, and I also watched its anime adaptation.
